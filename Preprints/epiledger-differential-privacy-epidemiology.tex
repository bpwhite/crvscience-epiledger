\begin{abstract}
EpiLedger (v2.0.0) is an open-source public health informatics workstation designed so local clinics, epidemiologists, and regional health agencies can track infectious outbreaks accurately while protecting individual patient confidentiality. Releasing small-town case counts or cross-linking clinic databases poses severe re-identification hazards, yet withholding public health summaries blinds schools and hospitals when transmission accelerates. EpiLedger bridges privacy and surveillance inside one offline-first application uniting live CDC SODA API ingestion (data.cdc.gov NNDSS and NWSS wastewater endpoints) alongside local CSV and HL7/FHIR clinical feeds, Laplace differential privacy with strict cumulative budget accountants, SHA-256 tamper-evident release ledgers, encrypted Bloom-filter record linkage, NegEx clinical triage note screening, Cori Rt tracking, and wastewater variant deconvolution. Using 253 weeks of publicly aggregated, already de-identified CDC NNDSS U.S. measles tables as a realistic ground-truth benchmark for privatizing sensitive internal clinic line-lists prior to release, differential-privacy noise stayed under one case per week (MAE = 0.972), ledger hashing flagged simulated single-cell edits immediately, encrypted record matching scored F1 = 1.00, clinical filtering excluded all 37 negated mentions with zero false alerts, Cori estimation captured peak Rt = 4.72, and wastewater estimation resolved a JN.1-to-XEC transition cleanly (mean L1 error 0.00765).
\end{abstract}

\section{1. Introduction}
The Privacy-Versus-Timeliness Dilemma in Public Health: Whenever a contagious outbreak---whether measles, seasonal influenza, respiratory syncytial virus, or an emerging viral variant---begins circulating across schools, clinics, and neighborhoods, county epidemiologists, school nurses, and hospital preparedness officers need reliable weekly situational clarity rapidly. Three urgent practical questions anchor almost every morning public health briefing: Is local transmission speeding up or cooling down, measured through the instantaneous reproduction number R t where values above 1.0 warn that each infected resident passes illness to more than one successor [1] ? Are hospital intake notes capturing genuine acute symptoms rather than reassuring negative statements such as "patient denies fever or cough" [3] ? And which viral lineages are rising inside neighborhood sewage treatment samples before clinical hospitalizations crest [2] ? Why Informal Redaction Fails Under Cross-Tabulation: Sharing granular clinic figures from small towns or sparsely populated rural zip codes, however, introduces severe patient privacy risks. Classic informal masking rules---such as hiding table cells containing fewer than five cases---repeatedly unravel under basic cross-tabulation differencing: outside observers subtract neighboring urban figures from regional subtotals or line up consecutive weekly bulletins to deduce masked small-town counts cleanly. Equally challenging, reconciling duplicate clinic lab tests across independent hospitals historically pressured teams either to trade plaintext patient names over insecure spreadsheets or tolerate double-counted patients inflating perceived surges. Modern privacy engineering resolves both dilemmas rigorously without blinding epidemic detection. Laplace differential privacy adds precisely scaled mathematical jitter managed by a strict finite query budget so any single patient's participation stays provably obscured; SHA-256 hash chaining constructs tamper-evident public logs proving released tables were never silently doctored after publication; and keyed Bloom-filter record linkage compares patient records across separated clinics completely blindly. EpiLedger packages these cryptographic guarantees directly beside real-time outbreak tracking offline.

\section{2. Architecture \& Methods}
Calibrated Differential Privacy, Audit Chains, and Blind Record Matching: EpiLedger (v 2.0.0 ) maintains all sensitive clinic feeds strictly inside local health-department custody without third-party cloud transfers. At the privacy-stewardship layer in epi-store , dp\_and\_audit.rs applies calibrated Laplace statistical jitter proportional to maximum single-resident table contribution while debiting a cumulative privacy-loss accountant: once combined ε spending touches configured agency limits, unbudgeted follow-up queries halt automatically so repeated sampling cannot average noise out to zero. Simultaneously, every public data release chains cryptographically via SHA-256 digests so external auditors flag any altered cell instantaneously. When regional clinic partners must merge shared patients without disclosing names, birthdates, or addresses, pprl\_bloom.rs conducts privacy-preserving record linkage (PPRL): participating clinics scramble text fragments locally into salted Bloom bit arrays, skip mismatched strangers rapidly via compact MinHash blocking signatures, and reconcile overlapping individuals accurately without exchanging plaintext identifiers. Syndromic Screening, Live CDC SODA Ingestion, and Wastewater Deconvolution: Upstream data adapters in epi-ingest ( cdc\_soda.rs and syndromic\_essence.rs ) connect directly to live public CDC SODA REST endpoints on data.cdc.gov ---fetching National Notifiable Diseases Surveillance System (NNDSS) weekly case tables and National Wastewater Surveillance System (NWSS) viral telemetry feeds---alongside importing local CSV files and HL7/FHIR clinical hospitalization feeds while filtering triage prose through NegEx linguistic negation parsing [3] so phrases like "ruled out measles" do not trigger false alerts. Downstream in epi-analytics , renewal\_rt.rs and nowcast.rs convert weekly counts into delay-adjusted curves and solve Bayesian Cori R t trajectories [1] , while wastewater\_demix.rs resolves sewage sequencing frequencies into circulating variant shares [2] inside desktop cartograms and CLI runners. \section{3. Capabilities \& Benchmark Results}
Low-Distortion Privacy Calibration on Public NNDSS Baseline Series: To test how accurately differential privacy preserves epidemic dynamics before clinics apply it to sensitive internal clinical line-lists prior to release, EpiLedger was benchmarked against 253 weeks of publicly aggregated, already de-identified CDC NNDSS U.S. measles surveillance series---deliberately stressed across 90 low-count intervals reporting nine or fewer weekly cases where re-identification pressure would be highest in raw clinical exports. Injecting Laplace jitter tuned at ε = 0.75 altered weekly totals by under a single case on average (MAE = 0.9723 cases, RMSE = 1.6078), keeping major outbreak spikes clearly separated from quiet inter-epidemic stretches. Enforcing a cumulative ε cap of 2.0 immediately stopped an extra unauthorized budget-exceeding query, while SHA-256 ledger verification caught an injected single-cell tamper modification instantaneously right at sequence index 1. Accurate Record Linkage, Zero False Alerts, Peak R t , and Variants: Across partner-clinic record reconciliation spanning 80 noisy synthetic patient profiles carrying typographical variations and 40 genuine clinic overlaps, MinHash blocking skipped 91.77\% of unnecessary pair checks while accurately recovering every single overlapping resident (F1 = 1.00). Evaluating emergency-triage prose across 68 clinical notes, NegEx linguistic screening isolated all 37 negated symptom mentions cleanly with zero false positives [3] . Feeding those protected tables directly into outbreak analytics revealed vivid epidemiological dynamics: Cori renewal modeling [1] traced the full 253-week measles series smoothly, quantifying an acute transmission burst reaching R t = 4.72 (95\% credible interval 3.23--6.49 across 129 valid sliding-window weeks with n >= 12) before cooling safely below unity to R t = 0.72 under public-health intervention. Concurrently, wastewater allele deconvolution [2] resolved a 10-week sewage transition cleanly from SARS-CoV-2 variant JN.1 (56.7\% share in Week 1) to XEC (54.0\% in Week 10) within mean L1 error 0.00765---archived inside cori\_rt\_and\_dp\_audit.csv . Think of an ε privacy budget like a community's protective confidentiality jar guarded across an entire respiratory season. Each time an analyst releases a weekly zip-code chart or county breakdown, a measured fraction of that budget gets spent permanently proportional to how sharply one resident's hospital visit could shift reported totals. Unlike naive hand-erased tables that curious outsiders routinely crack by comparing municipal counts against regional totals, calibrated Laplace jitter backed by strict budget enforcement makes any individual resident's participation mathematically indistinguishable---while SHA-256 hash chaining seals every published release publicly so silent edits fail audit immediately. Likewise, translating linkage and clinical screening outputs into plain words clarifies their everyday public-health impact. Pruning 91.77\% of fruitless pairwise checks with MinHash allows modest town servers to match overlapping clinic patients cleanly across hospital boundaries in fractions of a second without exposing names, while NegEx filtering spares health officers from waking up to bogus fever spikes triggered solely by negative emergency chart phrases. \section{4. Applications \& Use Cases}
County Health Departments and Multi-Clinic Collaborations: County and municipal public health teams balance twin public obligations every week: keeping resident medical privacy legally inviolate while delivering honest local outbreak guidance to school boards, clinics, and assisted-living facilities on tight budgets. Running EpiLedger natively on local office machines lets small agencies deduplicate multi-clinic testing blindly through Bloom linkage, monitor unbiased Cori R t bounds [1] and sewage variant shifts [2] , and share weekly charts anchored by auditable SHA-256 integrity logs and finite privacy budgets. Because every file stays strictly offline inside institutional walls, even part-time district nurses run complete weekly dispatches without remote cloud vendor delays or legal compliance headaches. Training Public Health Students in Data Ethics and Outbreak Math: In biostatistics and public-health informatics seminars, EpiLedger makes data-governance theory concrete and visual. Students observe firsthand why small ε budgets limit repeated queries, how NegEx linguistic rules spare syndromic dashboards from false hospital alarms [3] , and how credible interval bands widen naturally whenever weekly incidence stays low. Hospital Infection Control, School Districts, and Wastewater Alerts: Beyond county epidemiology desks, hospital infection-control nurses, municipal water utilities, and regional school districts increasingly watch the same local respiratory or gastrointestinal wave from totally different angles. When wastewater sampling plants detect rising viral signals days before pediatric clinics see fever spikes, keeping partners aligned usually stalls behind incompatible spreadsheets and strict medical privacy boundaries that bar emailing patient rosters across town. EpiLedger gives non-specialist staff a shared offline common language: duplicate visits across urgent-care centers collapse cleanly behind anonymized tokens, wastewater concentration curves sit cleanly beside clinic trends on one synchronized timeline, and smoothed reproduction estimates reveal whether community transmission is genuinely accelerating or simply catching up after a weekend holiday reporting gap. Building Public Trust Through Verifiable Privacy Accounting: Ordinary residents, patient advocates, and city council members understandably ask whether community outbreak charts could accidentally reveal sensitive health details inside smaller towns or rural zip codes. By enforcing fixed privacy noise budgets directly inside every exported aggregate report and sealing transformation histories under cryptographic audit hashes, EpiLedger transforms vague legal privacy promises into concrete, inspectable safeguards that local oversight boards can verify independently without specialist cryptography degrees. Paired alongside ecological, demographic, and clinical companions in the public collection at crvscience.com/apps , the desktop suite lowers technical barriers so smaller municipalities gain disciplined, transparent epidemic situational awareness while keeping sensitive community records under strict local stewardship. Clear uncertainty reporting matters equally when speaking to reporters and worried families: showing why small rural towns carry wider reproduction uncertainty bands discourages misleading single-day headlines while honoring real biological caution. Instead of treating statistical epidemiology as an opaque black box accessible only to state agencies, clear desktop workflows empower regional clinic partnerships to explain health recommendations calmly using verifiable local evidence.
